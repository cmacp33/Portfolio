RL Underactuated Watercraft

Objective

The objective of this project is to design, build, and control small solar-powered model watercraft to investigate reinforcement learning–based control of underactuated boats. The focus is on developing and testing controllers for tasks such as station-keeping, docking, and collision avoidance under environmental disturbances.
