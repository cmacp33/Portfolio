UBC Solar - Design Team


About

UBC Solar is a student design team focused on designing and racing a solar powered car. The team usually competes at one or two major competitions each year, including the Formula Sun Grand Prix (FSGP) and the American Solar Challenge (ASC). I'm a member of the Power and Signals team which manages all of our electrical systems apart from the main battery. My contributions to the team have helped develop my skills in embedded programming, motor simulation/control, functional safety analysis, and PCB design, assembly, and validation.

Sprint Kayak - Athletics

    About

    I’ve competed in sprint kayaking since I was seven years old. My dedication to the sport has led to many successes, most notably earning me a spot on the U16 National Team in 2019 and the U18 National Team in 2021. My typical training schedule consisted of 2–3 training sessions per day and included activities such as paddling (obviously), running, strength training, HIIT, and swimming. I’ve also competed in a few other sports including track, cross-country, basketball, and soccer. My best results outside of kayaking came from soccer where my team was consistently ranked 1st or 2nd at the provincial championships from 2016-2019. From my time in sports I’ve gotten very good at balancing a busy schedule and using my time efficiently. I’ve also learned to work well in high-pressure situations as races would often come down to hundredths or thousandths of a second, leaving absolutely no room for error.
